## Deep Cascade census correction

The executable loop is:

```text
12 dimensions * 6 bodies * 6 cycles * 6 agents * 6 trinities * 6 inferences
= 93,312 scalar beat evaluations per 12-dimension wave
```

The 37-dimension traversal runs four padded waves:

```text
4 * 93,312 = 373,248 beat evaluations per candidate
```

This measured execution census is separate from the operator-specified
`93,312 topology_nodes_per_pass` axis. A receipt must retain both names and may
not promote repeated heuristic evaluations into distinct trained nodes.

## Known collision and stub boundaries

- The inspected 1,024-alphabet BPI codec carries 60 bits of a 64-bit value,
  creating a 16-way namespace collapse. It is not reusable for exact identity
  until the four missing bits are carried or deterministically recovered.
- The HBP reader verifies receipts, but its quant decode is a passthrough stub.
- The garbage collector is a retention/demotion contract and 2,000-emission
  callback, not a data-aware deduplicating codec.
- The Fischer kernel is a deterministic policy/anti-blunder scorer, not a
  substitute for the residual stream.
- The existing White Room and glyph draft surfaces do not yet constitute a
  learned, standalone noun/verb/word language.
